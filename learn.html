Cowrywise Financial Academy

Learn money terms simplified

No jargon, no confusing formulas. Clear explanations of savings, investing, and personal finance concepts to help you make smarter money moves.

Asset Allocation
An investment strategy that aims to balance risk and reward by dividing your portfolio's assets among various asset classes, such as cash, fixed income, mutual funds, and equities.
Investing Strategy
Bull Market vs Bear Market
A Bull Market occurs when asset and stock prices are rising and investor confidence is high. A Bear Market occurs when prices drop by 20% or more from recent highs amid economic caution.
Market Cycles
Compound Interest
Interest calculated on the initial principal and also on all previously accumulated interest. Albert Einstein famously called compound interest the eighth wonder of the world.
Wealth Fundamentals
Diversification
The practice of spreading your investments across different industries, assets, and companies so that the poor performance of any single asset does not wipe out your total wealth.
Risk Management
Emergency Fund
A readily accessible pool of savings—typically 3 to 6 months of living expenses—set aside strictly to cover unforeseen financial emergencies like job loss, medical bills, or urgent car repairs.
Savings
Fixed Income
An investment type that provides periodic interest payments and returns principal at maturity, such as Treasury Bills, Federal Government Bonds, and corporate commercial paper.
Assets
Inflation
The gradual increase in prices of goods and services over time, which reduces the purchasing power of your money. Investing in assets that yield higher than inflation preserves your real wealth.
Economics
Liquidity
The ease with which an asset can be converted into ready cash without negatively affecting its market value. Stash wallet funds are highly liquid; real estate is illiquid.
Cash Flow
Mutual Funds
An investment vehicle pooled from many investors that is professionally managed by an SEC-licensed fund manager to purchase a diversified portfolio of securities.
Investing
Return on Investment (ROI)
A performance measure used to evaluate the efficiency and profitability of an investment, expressed as a percentage of the initial cost.
Performance
SEC (Securities and Exchange Commission)
The primary regulatory agency overseeing capital markets and investment managers in Nigeria, ensuring investor protection, transparency, and market integrity.
Regulation
Yield
The income return on an investment, such as the interest or dividends received from holding a specific security, typically expressed as an annualized percentage.
Returns
